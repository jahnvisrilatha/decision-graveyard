          Architecture Overview

          The 7-Step Organizational Decision Pipeline

          Every proposal is evaluated systematically against persistent corporate history

          1

          Historical decisions are stored in organizational memory

          Past successes, failures, abandoned initiatives, and rejected proposals are indexed as structured documents in Hindsight Cloud, preserving lessons, constraints, and outcome drivers.

          2

          Hindsight recalls relevant memories

          When a Product Manager submits a proposal, the system builds semantic recall queries capturing functional intent, architecture choices, and business problems to retrieve pertinent memories.

          3

          The agent compares the current proposal with historical decisions

          The DecisionAgent inspects conceptual resemblance, assessing whether the proposal touches problem spaces or technical patterns the organization has explored before.

          4

          GhostDetector identifies potential historical overlap

          If a proposal directly resembles an abandoned or failed project, GhostDetector flags the match with a qualitative confidence rating and extracts historical blockers.

          5

          MultiProposalDetector identifies composite ghosts

          Teams often decompose large initiatives into innocent micro-features. MultiProposalDetector evaluates whether separate proposals collectively recreate an abandoned macro-project.

          6

          The system compares historical blockers with current conditions

          The Then vs. Now audit balances the analysis by isolating blockers that still apply from those that have changed favorably (e.g. mobile adoption growing from 18% to 76%).

          7

          The Product Manager makes the final decision

          The system is strictly consultative. It equips the Product Manager with probing questions and evidence, leaving the final business judgment in human hands.

          Enterprise Architecture & Safeguards

          Engineered to eliminate hallucinations and support explainability

          👁 Grounded in Real Memory

          Hindsight Cloud serves as the external source of truth. The LLM cannot hallucinate prior decisions that do not exist in organizational memory.

          🚫 No Fake Similarity Percentages

          Avoids misleading numeric scores (e.g. "84.7% similar"). Instead, relies on qualitative confidence tiers (High, Medium, Low) backed by explicit conceptual reasoning.

          ⚖ Purely Consultative Standard

          The agent never issues imperative commands like "Reject this proposal." It highlights risk persistence and asks probing questions.
